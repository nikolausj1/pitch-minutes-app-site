Support

Developer: Justin Nikolaus. For help or a problem report, use the app’s public support tracker.

Contact the developer

Support reports are public. Do not include player names, private screenshots, or roster backups. Describe the problem using fictional names and include the app version and iOS version.

How do I start?

Add players in Roster. Set the team and match defaults in Settings. In Games, create a match, check attendance, and choose the formation. Tap empty positions and bench players to set the lineup, then start at the whistle.

How do substitutions work?

Tap a field player and a bench player in either order. Tap two field players to exchange positions. Tap a field player and the Bench heading to bring them off. Undo restores the last lineup.

Does the clock stop at zero?

No. Stop the period at the whistle. Locking the phone or backgrounding the app does not stop match time. Match tools can correct a late lineup change or accidental period end.

What do the colors mean?

Green is a fresh shift. Yellow begins at 60% of the target. The clock icon means the target has been reached. Bench order prioritizes least-played players, then longest rest.

How do I keep my records safe?

In Settings, export a backup to a location you control. Backups include names. Restore adds missing records without replacing existing records. A second unfinished game cannot be restored until the current game is finished or discarded.

How do I delete my records?

Delete individual games from Games, or choose Delete this team’s data in Settings. Archive preserves a player’s history and Restore returns that player. Delete exported files separately wherever you saved them.

Where is my data?

On this device. There is no team cloud-sync service. Apple device backups may include app data according to your device settings. Export before uninstalling or moving phones.
